## [1.0.0] - 2026-06-10

First alpha release of Moog's Mineshafts Reimagined.

### Added
- Six biome-specific reimagined mineshafts: base (taiga and similar overworld biomes), desert, jungle, mesa, snowy, and nether. A different mineshaft generates depending on the biome above it.
- Hand-built jigsaw tunnels and rooms made from vanilla blocks and entities, with loot, mob spawners, and feature rooms (desert quarry, archaeology dig, stable and vault; jungle brewing room and hidden furnace door; nether mess hall and a rare warden corridor; and more).
- Themed loot variants for supply barrels, miner chests, and treasure so containers vary between rooms, plus vaults and trial spawners.
- Config datapack download so players can fine-tune structure settings.

### Notes
- Requires Moog's Structure Lib.
- Version-gated structure pieces: 1.21.9+ blocks degrade gracefully to equivalents on 1.21 through 1.21.8.
